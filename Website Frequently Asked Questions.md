### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How does DogWalkr connect with Strava?**
DogWalkr seamlessly syncs with your Strava account. You track your walks, hikes, or runs on Strava as usual, and DogWalkr automatically imports the canine-specific data without you having to run two separate GPS trackers.

**2. How do I make sure my walk attributes to my dog?**
Simply add your dog's name hashtag in your Strava walk title or description (e.g., `#Audrey` or `#Daisy`). DogWalkr detects the tag and logs the walk straight to their profile.

**3. What if I walk multiple dogs at once?**
Just add all their hashtags to the Strava activity (e.g., `#Audrey #Joe`). DogWalkr automatically attributes the walk, elapsed time, and paw steps to every tagged dog in your pack.

**4. Why does DogWalkr use elapsed time instead of moving time?**
Because dog walks involve sniffs, stops, and greetings! Human moving time auto-pauses when you stop to let your dog explore. DogWalkr uses total elapsed time so your dog gets full credit for all their outdoor enrichment time.

**5. How do I share the walk cards to Instagram Stories?**
Open any completed walk in the app, tap **Export Story Card**, and choose between the **Transparent Sticker** or **Photo Overlay**. Tap Share to send it straight to your Instagram Stories via your iPhone share sheet.

**6. What are "Bones"?**
Bones are DogWalkr’s version of Strava Kudos. When friends in your pack log an activity, tap the 🦴 icon to give them a cheer!
